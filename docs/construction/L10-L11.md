# T.Pay L10-L11 construction

## L10 — Organization OS
Tenant-safe organization lifecycle, roles/permissions, member state, short-lived organization sessions and standard organization surfaces are now represented as durable contracts. Financial authority remains separate: an organization role can request/approve only through explicit permission gates; the AI layer cannot bypass these controls.

## L11 — AI Core
The AI Core is a policy-governed orchestration layer, not a chat wrapper. Agent roles, tool registry, risk classification, human approval gates, provider routing and AI-run audit records are defined. Sensitive/financial data defaults to an internal safety route. External providers such as OpenAI/Anthropic remain replaceable adapters and require explicit data policy; no credential or vendor access is fabricated.

## Togo/Africa additions
Cash/offline workflows remain outside AI authority; GPS is evidence rather than payment authorization; USSD/SMS/voice adapters should invoke the same policy engine; low-bandwidth clients receive deterministic compact responses; French/Ewe/Kabiyè localization belongs above the policy layer.

## Status
L10-L11 are **IN PROGRESS**. Production repositories, HTTP/USSD/SMS/voice adapters, PostgreSQL race proofs, model-provider contract suites, observability and deployment are still required.

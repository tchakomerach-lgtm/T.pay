# T.Pay L10-L11 construction

## L10 — Organization OS
- Durable organization lifecycle, role/permission model and tenant isolation.
- Short organization sessions (default 15 minutes) with revocation/expiry checks.
- Organization types cover the defined Togo/Africa vertical catalog.
- Standard surfaces are modeled once: public page, website, internal social, marketplace, recruitment, documents, communications and wallet.
- Financial approval remains an explicit permission; organization membership never becomes implicit Ledger authority.

## L11 — AI Core
- Super-Orchestrator contract separates reasoning, tools, policy and execution.
- Tool risk classes and human-approval gates prevent direct high-impact execution.
- Financial/sensitive data defaults to an internal safety route.
- Provider adapters are replaceable; OpenAI/Anthropic are optional provider implementations, not hard-coded dependencies. No unavailable credentials or external access is fabricated.
- Prompt-injection detection, request-size limits, organization scoping and security/audit events are first-class controls.
- USSD/SMS/voice/mobile clients must enter through the same policy layer; offline state is evidence/intents and cannot become financial truth.

## Remaining exit gates
L10-L11 remain IN PROGRESS until PostgreSQL race tests, production repositories/handlers, provider contract tests, real model adapter abstraction, observability, rate/cost controls, secrets management, mobile/USSD/SMS/voice adapters and deployment are implemented and validated.

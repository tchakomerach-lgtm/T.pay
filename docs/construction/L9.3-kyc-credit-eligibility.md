# L9.3 — KYC / credit eligibility foundation

Implemented:
- progressive KYC profile/evidence schema;
- tenant-scoped credit applications and decision evidence;
- deterministic exposure and debt-service eligibility policy;
- human-review default for KYC gaps, delinquency and high risk;
- explicit partner-disbursement boundary;
- adversarial tests for unsafe automatic approval;
- CI trigger corrected to include pull requests.

Not implemented:
- real identity providers, biometric/liveness network calls;
- credit bureau or partner exposure APIs;
- actual loan disbursement/repayment settlement;
- school-finance orchestration;
- production regulatory authorization.

The brick is not complete until its exact-head CI check is green.
